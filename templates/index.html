About Our App

Our barcode scanner app is an innovative solution developed by the Business Affairs Committee of the Wentworth Student Government.

Our Mission

Designed specifically for college students, this app offers a quick and reliable way to navigate Tessie's market—empowering you to check product prices at a glance.

Technology

Built with Python, JavaScript, and HTML, the app combines state-of-the-art scanning technology with a user-friendly interface to ensure smooth and accurate performance.
